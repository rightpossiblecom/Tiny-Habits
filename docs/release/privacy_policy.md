# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** February 2026

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Tiny Habits is a strictly local application. We **do not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data**. 

## 2. Local Storage
All your habits, preferences, and completion histories are stored entirely offline on the local storage (Hive database) of your device. This data is not sent to any servers, and we have no access to it.

## 3. Account Requirements
There is no login, signup, or account creation required to use Tiny Habits. 

## 4. Updates to this Policy
If we decide to change our privacy policy, we will post those changes on this page. However, our commitment to zero data collection remains core to this application.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@tinyhabits.app.
