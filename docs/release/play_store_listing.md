Tiny Habits - Play Store Listing

Short Description
Track small daily habits consistency. Simple, minimal daily tracker.

Full Description
Tiny Habits is a minimal app that helps you track small daily actions. 
Build consistency with a frictionless interface. 
No coaching, no productivity systems, no analytics language. Just tap when done.

Features:
- Add your small daily routines
- Swipe to delete them
- Check them off with a single satisfying tap 
- View your 7-day consistency grid 
- Light and Dark mode themes

Privacy First:
Everything is stored directly on your phone. We do not collect, share, or track any personal data. Tiny Habits is simple, safe, and entirely yours.
