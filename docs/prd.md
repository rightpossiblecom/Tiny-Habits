

# Simple App Plan (Non-Technical)

## App Goal

**Tiny Habits** is a minimal app that helps users track small daily actions.

No coaching.
No health claims.
No productivity systems.

Just tap when done.

---

## What The App Does

* User adds small habits (e.g., “Drink water”)
* User taps habit to mark it done for the day
* Simple weekly view (7-day grid)
* Local storage only

That’s it.

---

## What It Does NOT Do

* No AI
* No reminders (v1)
* No health tracking
* No step counting
* No integration with wearables
* No medical or wellness claims
* No analytics dashboards

This keeps it simple and safe.

---

# Core User Story

> As a user, I open the app, see my small habits, tap the ones I completed, and close the app.

Quick. Frictionless.

---

# App Pages (Minimal Structure)

## 1️⃣ Main Screen (Primary Page)

Top:

* Today’s date

Middle:

* List of habits

  * Habit name
  * Circle checkbox
  * Tap → marked done

Bottom:

* “Add Habit” button

Limit habits to maybe 5–7 max to keep it minimal.

---

## 2️⃣ Weekly View Page

Very simple grid:

Habit name →
Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Sat Sun
✔ ✔ ✖ ✔ ✔ ✖ ✔

No charts.
No percentages.
No analytics language.

Just visual consistency.

---

## 3️⃣ Settings Page

Minimal:

* Reset all data
* Dark mode toggle
* Privacy Policy
* App version

Enough for Play Store completeness.

---

# MVP Scope (Realistic 3–4 Hour Build)

### Must Have

* Add habit
* Delete habit
* Tap to mark done
* Store daily status
* Simple weekly grid view

### Optional

* Streak per habit
* Limit max habits
* Simple confetti animation

# Play Store Positioning (Important)

Use phrases like:

* “Track small daily habits”
* “Build consistency”
* “Simple daily tracker”
* “Minimal habit app”

Avoid:

* Health improvement
* Mental health
* Weight loss
* Addiction recovery
* “Scientifically proven”

Keep it neutral productivity.
